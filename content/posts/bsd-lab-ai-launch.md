BSD-LAB AI 平台现已正式开放使用。平台面向实验室成员提供统一的 AI 工作空间，帮助大家更便捷地把 AI 工具应用到学习、研究和日常工作中。

## 平台可以做什么？

BSD-LAB AI 支持多种常见任务，包括：

- 日常问答、想法讨论与资料梳理；
- 图片和文档阅读、信息提取与内容分析；
- 论文阅读、学术写作与语言修改辅助；
- 编程、代码调试和研究流程支持。

平台同时提供网页试用、Codex Desktop 和 Chatbox 等使用方式。大家可以根据任务类型和个人习惯选择合适的工作入口。

## 如何开始使用？

访问 **[https://ai.bsd-lab.org/](https://ai.bsd-lab.org/)**，按照首页的首次使用指南完成账号激活、个人 API 密钥创建和客户端选择，即可开始使用。个人密钥只应保存在自己的设备上，请勿与他人分享。

使用 AI 处理研究任务时，请继续核对原始文献、数据和代码结果。不要上传患者或被试信息、未公开研究数据以及其他不适合交由第三方模型处理的敏感材料。

欢迎实验室成员体验平台，并根据自己的学习和研究场景探索更高效的工作方式。

<!-- en -->
The BSD-LAB AI platform is now officially open. Built for lab members, it provides a unified AI workspace for learning, research, and everyday work.

## What can the platform help with?

BSD-LAB AI supports a range of common tasks, including:

- General question answering, idea development, and information organization;
- Reading images and documents, extracting information, and analyzing content;
- Paper reading, academic writing, and language revision support;
- Programming, code debugging, and research workflow assistance.

The platform offers several ways to work, including a web interface, Codex Desktop, and Chatbox. Lab members can choose the option that best fits each task and working style.

## How do I get started?

Visit **[https://ai.bsd-lab.org/](https://ai.bsd-lab.org/)** and follow the instructions on the homepage to activate your account, create a personal API key, and select a client. Keep your personal key on your own device and do not share it with others.

When using AI for research, continue to verify claims against the original literature and check data and code outputs carefully. Do not upload patient or participant information, unpublished research data, or other sensitive materials that should not be processed by third-party models.

We invite all lab members to try the platform and explore how it can support more effective learning and research.
